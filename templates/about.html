About Us

School Practice Platform is a free online learning platform designed to help students practice quizzes and strengthen their knowledge in a simple and stress-free way.

Our goal is simple:

  • Provide free educational tools for students
  • Make learning easy, engaging, and accessible
  • Help students practice and improve without pressure

This platform is built and maintained independently with the intention of supporting both students and teachers in their learning journey.

We believe that education should be simple, free, and available to everyone.
